# Company context for the Industry desk

## KHD Technology
Keith Handy Design Ltd, Stone, Staffordshire, UK. https://khdtechnology.com

Designs and manufactures portable, food-grade **conveyor belt cleaning systems** that use **dry saturated steam** instead of chemicals.

- **Mesh belt cleaners:** Jet System 5, Static Spinner, Hand-Held Spinner
- **Portable flat belt cleaners:** Brush System, Brushless System
- **Fixed flat belt cleaners:** Single Fixed Head, Dual Fixed Head
- **Special design** belt cleaners
- **Steam generators:** KHD-16 (9 kW, 16 A), KHD-32 (18 kW, 32 A), KHD-36 (36 kW, 63 A)
- **Factory wall cleaner:** telescopic carbon-fibre steam mop, 3.2 kg, reaches 8–9 m

Markets: food processing, especially **bakery** (bread lines, pans and trays), plus non-food uses. Belt types: wire mesh, spiral and modular. Sells direct in the UK and through distributors abroad.

Selling points: clean-in-place on the line, less labour, less water, energy and chemical use, fewer belt replacements, no pneumatics or complex controls.

## Main competitor: Goodway Technologies
Stamford, Connecticut, USA. Founded 1966. ISO 9001:2015. https://www.goodway.com

Much broader range than KHD: tube cleaning, industrial vacuums, cooling tower and HVAC maintenance, coil cleaning, descaling, dry steam cleaners. Food and beverage line includes the **PureBelt** conveyor belt cleaning system and **BioSpray** sanitising sprayers.

## What the Industry desk looks for
- Goodway news, launches and case studies (especially PureBelt) and news from the competitors above
- Food safety regulation and enforcement: UK FSA, BRCGS standard updates, US FDA FSMA
- Listeria, Salmonella and allergen recalls linked to equipment or line hygiene
- Bakery and food-processing investment: new plants, line upgrades, automation, labour shortages
- Sustainability pressure on water, energy and chemical use in sanitation
- Trade shows: PPMA Show, IFFA, Anuga FoodTec, IBIE, Process Expo
- Distributors' markets: food manufacturing news in countries where KHD has distributors
